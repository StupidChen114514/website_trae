Point of view

Shanghai / Everywhere

Motion is not decoration. It is the grammar of an interface — the pause before a decision, the weight of a tap, the colour that tells you something has changed. I design those moments, then I build them in the browser.

我把动效当作信息的时间结构：先决定节奏，再设计画面。 作品横跨品牌动态系统、WebGL 体验与产品界面动效， 从第一帧分镜到最后一行动画代码，全部亲手完成。

What I actually do

One pair of hands from the first sketch to the shipped build. 从概念到上线，不外包任何一个关键帧。

  • Timing curves, choreography and transition logic documented as a system your engineers can actually ship — not a folder of one-off animations.

    • Design tokens
    • Prototyping
    • Handoff

  • Custom GLSL for identities and experiences that could never exist as a video file. Real-time, reactive, alive on every machine that opens it.

    • GLSL
    • Three.js
    • Performance

  • Product films, look development and camera language built for the small screen — where a two-second shot has to sell the whole object.

    • Look dev
